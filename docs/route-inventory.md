# Route Inventory

Public marketing and account routes replicated against https://stormfiber.com/

## Main navigation
- `/` Home
- `/plans` Plans catalog
- `/plans/compare` Plan comparison
- `/plans/[slug]` Plan detail
- `/packages/[slug]` Plan detail alias
- `/products` Product hub
- `/products/ultra-fast-internet`
- `/products/hdtv`
- `/products/crystal-clear-voice`

## Onboarding
- `/get-stormfiber` Connection request
- `/get-stormfiber-2` Redirects to `/get-stormfiber`
- `/check-availability` Coverage checker

## Support & legal
- `/support` Billing (reference Support → Billing)
- `/support/billing` Redirects to `/support`
- `/support/get-in-touch` Contact
- `/support/contact` Redirects to `/support/get-in-touch`
- `/support/help-center` Redirects to `/faqs`
- `/faqs`
- `/offers`
- `/terms-and-conditions`
- `/privacy-policy`
- `/fair-usage-policy`
